Deploy Inkprint

Deploys contracts/Inkprint.sol (Solidity 0.8.20, optimizer 200 runs, EVM paris) from your MetaMask account. Deploy to testnet first, try the app, then deploy to mainnet.

Ready. You will confirm one transaction in MetaMask.
  1. After deploying, open the app on this same localhost address (add ?net=testnet for testnet). It picks up the new address automatically.
  2. For the live site, paste the address into the matching contract: '' field in CONFIG.networks near the top of app.js, and into the README.
